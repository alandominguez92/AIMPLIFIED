ROI, CLV & Model Accuracy

CLV is how far the vig-free market moved toward each pick between when it logged and when the game started — the truest measure of a good bet. ROI is return on 1u-flat stakes (1u = 1% of bankroll); projection error is the average miss between projected and actual strikeouts.

How tonight's numbers are built

Every projection is a transparent rate model: a pitcher's strikeout rate scaled by his expected innings and the opposing lineup's strikeout tendency, then adjusted for park and game-time weather. The edge is the model's probability measured against the vig-free line — never a hunch.
