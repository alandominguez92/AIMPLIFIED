What would change our mind
  1. CLV first. If the market consistently moves toward our number after we post, we are early — that shows up long before profit does.
  2. Then separation. Buckets we call stronger should actually perform better. They do not yet, which is why the board no longer ranks them.
  3. Profit last. It is the noisiest of the three and the slowest to become significant.

In that order, deliberately. Profit arriving first without the other two is most likely variance, and we would rather be early than lucky.
