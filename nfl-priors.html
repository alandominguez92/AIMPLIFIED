NFL Priors

This is the input sheet, not a board. Every number here is 2025 play-by-play — how often a player was on the field, what share of his team's work he took, and what he did with it — held against the 2026 schedule. There is no fair line and no price on this page, so there is nothing to post and nothing to grade. It is here so the inputs can be checked before a projection is built on them.

What these priors can't tell you read before using a number
Nothing here is coloured for strength. A delta against the league rate renders grey in both directions — above average is not a bet, and this page has no prices to bet into.
